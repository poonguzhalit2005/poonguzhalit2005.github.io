About me

A calm, respectful space to be heard

I am an empathetic and ethically grounded M.Sc Clinical Psychology student at Rathinam Global University, Coimbatore. My training has taken me into a therapy and rehabilitation centre, a psychiatric hospital and a de-addiction clinic, where I learned to take careful case histories, conduct Mental Status Examinations and support patients and families through psychoeducation.

I believe good care starts with listening — and is strengthened by structured assessment and evidence-based methods such as CBT. I'm working toward becoming a licensed Clinical Psychologist.

"Every person deserves to be understood before they are helped."

How I work

My approach to care

Grounded in supervised clinical practice, my work brings together careful assessment, evidence-based therapy and support for the people around the client.

Assessment

Clinical case history, Mental Status Examination, psychometric assessment and clear psychological reports.

Therapeutic support

CBT-informed and supportive counseling, with motivational interviewing to build readiness for change.

Psychoeducation

Helping patients and families understand conditions, treatment and coping — individually and in groups.

Rehabilitation

Supporting recovery in rehabilitation and de-addiction settings alongside multidisciplinary teams.
