How I work

My approach to care

Grounded in supervised clinical practice, my work brings together careful assessment, evidence-based therapy and support for the families and teams around each person.

Assessment

Detailed case history interviews, Mental Status Examination, psychometric assessment and accurate psychological reports.

Therapeutic support

CBT-informed and supportive counseling, individually and in groups, with motivational interviewing to build readiness for change.

Psychoeducation

Helping patients and families understand conditions, treatment and coping strategies.

Rehabilitation

Supporting recovery and rehabilitation planning in therapy and de-addiction settings, alongside multidisciplinary teams.

Therapeutic approaches

Methods I am trained in

1

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

Working with the links between thoughts, feelings and behaviour to build healthier patterns and coping skills.

2

Supportive Counseling

A warm, non-judgemental space that strengthens a person's existing resources and resilience.

3

Psychoeducation-based Therapy

Clear information about conditions and treatment so patients and families can take an active part in recovery.

4

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative conversation style that helps people explore ambivalence and find their own reasons for change.
